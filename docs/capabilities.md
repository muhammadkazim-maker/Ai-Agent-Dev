## 2. Known Limitations & Honest Boundaries (Phase 05)
1. **Explicit Half-Duplex Policy (No Spoken Barge-In)**: The microphone is intentionally disabled during `THINKING` (`PROCESSING`) and `SPEAKING` to prevent acoustic echo feedback into the live session. Spoken barge-in is not claimed; operators can interrupt speech at any time using the large **Pause** or **Stop** buttons.
2. **Foreground-Only Execution**: Active conversation runs while the app is in the foreground. Backgrounding the activity (`ON_STOP` with `isChangingConfigurations == false`) releases microphone and audio playback hardware and closes the live socket cleanly.
3. **Zero Action Tools & No Accessibility Service in Phase 05**: `tools = emptyList()` (`registeredActionToolsCount = 0`), and no AccessibilityService or background call-control service is registered in Phase 05 per `PROJECT_CONTRACT.md`.
